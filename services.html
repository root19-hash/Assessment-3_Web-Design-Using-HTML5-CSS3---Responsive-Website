Our Printing Services

Matheson Library Printing Services offers a range of printing and photocopying options to help students, staff, and library users with their academic and personal printing needs.

Printing & Copying Services and Prices

The table below outlines our available printing and copying services along with their prices.

Printing & Copying Services and Prices
Service Price Description
Black and White Printing K0.50 / page Affordable black and white printing for assignments, notes, reports, study materials, and other documents.
Colour Printing K1.00 / page High-quality colour printing for presentations, posters, projects, and documents that need a professional appearance.
Photocopying K0.20 / page Photocopying services are available for study materials, documents, notes, and other suitable materials.

How to Use the Printing Service

  1. Prepare the document you want to print.
  2. Check the printing service, paper size, and print type you need.
  3. Complete the Print Request Form on our Booking & Contact page.
  4. Submit your printing request.
  5. Contact the library if you need assistance with your request.
